Appendix

Method, coverage, glossary and the full relationship table.

Suggested actions are mined from every connected source, matched against the revealed preferences, and scored where the picture is complete: score = fit (1–5) × distance (1–5) × timing (0–1) × feasibility (0.5–1), max 25. Higher distance means the two sides share no room, chain or circuit except you. Coverage notes & gaps
    Glossary — key definitions
    Bond
    The strength of a relationship, 0–100. Built from how much you actually talk (every message and meeting, weighted by channel — a WhatsApp counts three times an email), then boosted when the relationship runs across several channels, when the other person writes to you as much as you write to them, and when they have vouched for you or you for them.
    Heat
    How recently the relationship was alive, 0–1. Decays with months of silence; multi-channel relationships cool slowly (a year to fade), single-channel ones quickly (about three months).
    Rings
    Position in your orbit. Inner circle = strong bond and in touch now. Working orbit = active this quarter. Outer orbit = quiet for 3–12 months. Deep field = over a year of silence.
    Multiplexity
    The number of different channels a relationship lives on. Someone you email, WhatsApp and meet is harder to lose than someone who exists only in your inbox.
    Reciprocity
    Whether the traffic flows both ways. A relationship where they initiate as often as you do scores higher than one you carry alone.
    Vouched
    Someone who has introduced or recommended you, or you them — the strongest single signal of real trust in the corpus.
    Fit
    How well a suggested action matches what the person actually wants (1–5), always evidenced by their own words.
    Distance
    How far apart the two sides are (1–5). Maximum distance means they share no room, chain or circuit except you — which is where introductions create the most value.
    Timing
    Whether the moment is right (0–1). A dotted line in your orbit means the window is closing.
    Feasibility
    How likely the action is to actually happen (0.5–1), given what each side has said and how reachable they are.
    Revealed preference
    Something you demonstrably want — stated in your own words in the corpus, or inferred from repeated behaviour and confirmed with you.
    Suggested action
    One concrete step — an introduction, a reactivation, a repair, a decision — scored, evidenced and drafted, never sent without you.
    Dust
    The wider network: people indexed from all sources who sit outside the eight named clusters. Every dot is a real person; zoom in to see their names.
